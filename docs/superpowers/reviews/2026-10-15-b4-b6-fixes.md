## Limitations and compatibility

- Full browser acceptance remains unverified: the scoped attempt encountered occupied port 3001; the earlier review encountered a Next/Turbopack Google-font resolution error. No unrelated font/server changes were made.

- Real graph/action-store factories and real-run/citation UI handoffs remain deferred dependencies. These fixes do not turn those stubs into working integrations or complete B4/B6 deployed acceptance.
